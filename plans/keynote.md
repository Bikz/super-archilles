# Objective: The audience remembers one new capability, sees it work live, and knows when they can get it

## Opening (0.75 min) [must]
- One story about the problem customers hit every day
- The promise of this talk in one sentence

## What is new (1.5 min)
- The single headline capability
- Why it was not possible before

## Live demo (2.5 min) [must]
- Show the before and after on a real workflow
- Name the number that improved

## Customer proof (1.25 min)
- One customer quote and their result

## Availability and pricing (1 min) [must]
- Launch date, editions and price
- Where to sign up today

## Close (1 min) [must]
- Restate the one thing to remember
- Thank partners and the team
