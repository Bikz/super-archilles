# Objective: Board approves the Q3 results and the proposed acquisition next steps, and every decision has an owner and a date

## Call to order and prior minutes (3 min) [must]
- Quorum confirmed and conflicts of interest declared
- Approve minutes of the last meeting

## CEO update (7 min)
- Quarter highlights and misses against plan
- Top three priorities for next quarter

## Financial review (10 min) [must]
- Revenue, gross margin and burn against budget
- Cash runway and covenant headroom
- Audit committee report

## Strategic matter: proposed acquisition (12 min) [must]
- Target, price range and rationale
- Diligence findings and integration risks
- Disclosure and trading restrictions while talks continue

## Risk and compliance (6 min)
- Open litigation and regulatory inquiries
- Cybersecurity incidents this quarter

## Resolutions and action items (5 min) [must]
- Read out each resolution and record the vote
- Confirm owners and due dates for follow-ups

## Executive session (2 min)
- Independent directors only
