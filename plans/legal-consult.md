# Objective: Attorney understands the client's matter well enough to confirm scope, flag urgent deadlines, and agree next steps and fees

## Introductions and privilege (2 min) [must]
- Confirm who is in the room; third parties can waive privilege
- Explain that this conversation is confidential and privileged

## Conflict check (3 min)
- Names of all parties, companies and opposing counsel
- Any prior dealings between the firm and the other side

## Facts of the matter (10 min) [must]
- What happened, in order, with dates
- Key documents: contracts, emails, notices received
- Who else knows about this and what has been said to them

## Client goals and constraints (5 min)
- Outcome the client wants and what they can live with
- Budget, timing and business pressures

## Deadlines and exposure (5 min) [must]
- Limitation periods, response dates and court dates
- Money at stake and any insurance that may respond

## Engagement and next steps (5 min) [must]
- Scope of the engagement and fee arrangement
- Documents the client will send, and by when
- Litigation hold: preserve emails and files from today
