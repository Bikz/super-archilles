# Objective: Judges believe a live talk copilot running 100% on the GB10 is useful, real-time, and worth paying for

## Hook (0.5 min) [must]
- Every speaker drifts and runs over; nobody can watch their own clock and plan at once
- This copilot is listening to the talk right now

## Problem and who pays (0.75 min)
- Lecturers, corporate trainers, sales teams, all-hands presenters
- Classroom and meeting audio is private, so it can't go to a cloud API

## Live demo (1.5 min) [must]
- What the copilot does: catches drift, re-plans when behind, saves good tangents
- Point out the nudges arriving on Telegram and the dashboard as they happen

## How it runs on the GB10 (0.75 min) [must]
- NVIDIA streaming speech model plus a large language model, both on the box
- 128 GB unified memory, no cloud calls, keeps working with the wifi off

## Close (0.5 min) [must]
- The debrief arrives the moment the talk ends
- Ask: pilot with a training team
